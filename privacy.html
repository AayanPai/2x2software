Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit 2x2software.com. The short version: we don't collect personal information on this site. There are no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics.

1. Who we are

This website is run by 2×2 Software ("we", "us"). You can reach us at 2x2software.com@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers this website only. Our products, including Bifrost and Cadencely, have their own privacy policies that explain how they handle your data.

3. What we collect

We don't ask for, collect or store personal information through this site. It has no sign-up, no contact form, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers.

4. What's stored in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your browser remembers your choice in its local storage, under the name 2x2-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Services that see your visit

Like any website, this one relies on a few outside services. When you visit, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visits to keep the service secure and running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves some of the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Cloudflare answers the DNS lookups that turn 2x2software.com into an address. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We don't receive this information from them, and we don't use it to identify you.

6. Links to other sites

When you follow a link to another site, including our own products, that site's privacy policy applies.

7. Children

This site isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information from visits to this site, there's usually nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If you think we hold information about you, email us and we'll look into it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this site starts collecting information, for example through a contact form or analytics, we'll update this policy first and change the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email 2x2software.com@gmail.com.
