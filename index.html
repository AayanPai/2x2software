02How we work

Idea to product in four steps.
No skipping.

Step 01

Start with an idea

A problem we noticed, or a cool idea we can't shake. Either way, it has to be worth building.

Step 02

Keep it simple

Build the smallest version that makes the idea real. Then sand it down until it feels obvious.

Step 03

Ship it early

Put it in front of real people before it's perfect. They teach us more than planning ever will.

Step 04

Keep improving

Fix what's rough. Build on what works. A launch isn't the finish line. It's the start.
