[ How we work ]

Four steps from idea to product.
We repeat them every time.

Step 01

Start with an idea

Sometimes it's a problem we noticed. Sometimes it's just a cool idea we can't stop thinking about. Either way, it has to be worth building.

Step 02

Keep it simple

We build the smallest version that makes the idea real, then smooth it out until it feels obvious to use.

Step 03

Ship it early

We put it in front of real people before it's perfect. Watching them use it teaches us more than planning ever does.

Step 04

Keep improving

We fix what's rough and build on what works. A launch isn't the finish line. It's where the real work starts.
